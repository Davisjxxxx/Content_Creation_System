# FinOps and ROI Model

## Design principle
Text research is usually not the dominant cost. Generated video, avatar footage, repeated generations, and media services can dominate. Every provider call must be attributable to a story and subsystem.

## Initial policy encoded in `BudgetPolicy`
- monthly target: $250
- monthly hard planning ceiling: $500
- story soft limit: $40
- story hard limit: $60
- research soft allocation: $5
- certification: $3
- images: $5
- generated video: $25
- presenter: $8
- voice: $3

These are control defaults, not forecasts or vendor-price claims. Reprice providers before production rollout.

## Cost telemetry required
provider, model, story_id, agent/subsystem, units, generated seconds, retries/rerolls, latency, success, and USD cost.

## ROI attribution model
Track revenue and strategic value by content object:
- direct platform revenue
- affiliate revenue
- sponsorship attribution
- consulting lead attribution
- product/SaaS lead attribution
- subscriber/follower growth
- owned-site traffic when blog is added

## Core metrics
- cost per certified story
- cost per published long-form story
- cost per derivative asset
- cost per 1,000 views
- cost per subscriber/follower acquired
- cost per qualified consulting lead
- revenue / total content cost
- contribution margin after media/API costs

## Rule
Do not increase media-generation spend because output looks more cinematic. Increase it only when controlled tests show measurable improvement in retention, conversion, or strategic lead value.
