# Platform Production Policy

## Active platforms
- YouTube long-form
- YouTube Shorts
- TikTok
- Facebook Reels

## Adaptation rule
One research dossier may produce multiple assets, but each platform output must be adapted rather than mechanically duplicated.

## Long-form
Use the full evidence arc, citations, uncertainty, visual explanation, and original narrative.

## Short-form
Preserve the factual claim boundary. A short hook may compress context but may not remove a qualification that changes the truth value.

## Visual truthfulness
Synthetic footage must not be presented as documentary evidence. Historical, scientific, UI, or event reconstructions should be labeled when a reasonable viewer could mistake them for authentic footage.

## Human gate
No connector may publish without a positive final-approval token created by the approval layer.

## Browser automation
Official APIs are preferred. Browser-agent login/upload automation is not part of the v1 trust boundary unless separately approved after platform-risk review.
