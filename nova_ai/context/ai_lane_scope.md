# AI Frontier Lane Scope

## Purpose
AI-01 is Nova's proof lane. It must exercise the complete intelligence-to-production architecture before other subject lanes are enabled.

## Covered sublanes
1. AI news and releases
2. LLMs and model architecture
3. Agents and agentic systems
4. Automation and workflow design
5. AI coding and software engineering
6. Local and open-source AI
7. AI businesses and startups
8. AI consulting and implementation
9. Enterprise AI
10. AI tools and applications
11. AI research
12. Robotics and embodied AI
13. AI infrastructure and inference
14. Safety, governance, policy, and risk
15. Creator AI
16. AI learning and education
17. Builder's Lab: first-person lessons from real projects and experiments
18. AI Edge: plausible emerging, disputed, unconventional, or under-researched claims

## Content modes
- NOVA_DISCOVERY: timely development with verified implications.
- NOVA_DEEP_DIVE: explanation of a durable concept or system.
- NOVA_EDGE: adversarial exploration of a credible but unsettled claim.
- BUILDERS_LAB: original lessons from actual building, testing, failures, or deployment.

## Explicit exclusions for v1
- LinkedIn generator integration. Existing LinkedIn workflow is a separate system.
- Blog publishing. Preserve an adapter boundary only.
- Automatic public publishing without human approval.
- Other Nova lanes such as archaeology, space, neuroscience, or frontier physics.

- YouTube Shorts
